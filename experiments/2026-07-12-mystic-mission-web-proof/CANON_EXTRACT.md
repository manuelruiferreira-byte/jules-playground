# Canon Extract for This Experiment

Status: bounded approved input for an experiment, not a complete canon file.

## Allowed truths

- The public experience is called **Mystic Mission**.
- Reality contains a hidden layer.
- The central conflict is about remembrance rather than conquest.
- History or perception may have been edited.
- A damaged signal can cross from an erased or displaced timeline.
- **Kael** is the current protagonist name.
- Interaction may reveal approved story fragments.
- No agent may approve or promote canon.

## Withheld truths

Do not explain or definitively resolve:

- Erebus
- Kairoth
- SIPR
- the hidden sun
- the DMT threshold
- Sophia's full function
- Liora's full status
- the final timeline restoration

These may be hinted at only through abstract signal language, not explicit exposition.

## Naming law

Historical sources may contain `Juno`. Current public-facing output must use `Kael`.

## Experience law

Every physical or digital Mystic Mission environment should make the visitor feel that reality contains a hidden layer reacting to their presence. Interaction may reveal story material, but it may not invent or replace canon.

## Canon boundary

Everything produced in this experiment remains a candidate until Manuel explicitly approves promotion.
